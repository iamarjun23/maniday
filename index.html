How we work

Four stages. Your sign-off at each.

Every Manidhay project follows the same proven path. Select a stage to see what happens, what you receive and how involved you'll be.

01

Survey & feasibility

We walk the plot with you, run the soil test and boundary survey, and tell you honestly what the land can hold and roughly what it will cost, before a single line is drawn.

You receive
Site report, soil test results and an early budget estimate
Your involvement
One site visit with our engineer
02

Design

Architecture and structural drawings are developed together by one team, so the plan you approve is exactly what gets built, within budget and true to your vastu requirements.

You receive
Floor plans, elevations and structural drawings
Your involvement
Design reviews and a final sign-off
03

Approvals & finance

We prepare and file your plan sanction, NOCs and licences, follow them up with the authorities, and line up your home loan with stage-wise estimates the bank accepts.

You receive
Sanctioned plan, NOCs and a bank-ready estimate
Your involvement
Signing the applications; we handle the follow-up
04

Build & handover

The structure rises under engineer supervision, with every slab inspected before the next. Finishes are checked with you in a final walkthrough, and then the keys are yours.

You receive
Regular site updates, a final walkthrough, keys and documents
Your involvement
Stage sign-offs and the final walkthrough

Questions

Frequently asked.

Can't find your answer here? Call us and an engineer will talk you through it.

Do I have to take all seven services?

No. Many clients start with a single service, such as plan approval or structural design, and add others later. Every service can be taken on its own.

Is the first site visit really free?

Yes. We visit your plot, understand what you want to build and give you an honest early view of feasibility and budget, with no obligation.

Can you help me get a home loan?

Yes. We prepare bank-ready estimates and stage-wise cost plans, and work with your bank through the disbursement stages.

How do payments work during construction?

Payments are tied to completed stages, such as foundation, each slab and finishing, so you only pay for work that is done and inspected.

How long does a typical home take?

It depends on size, design and approvals. After the first site visit we give you a written, stage-by-stage schedule so you know exactly what to expect.

Which areas do you work in?

We work across Karnataka. Get in touch with your plot location and we'll confirm.
