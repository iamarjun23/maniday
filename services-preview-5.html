What we do

Seven services, one roof

Building usually means hiring an architect, a structural designer, an approvals agent, a loan consultant and a contractor, then managing all of them yourself. Choose one service from us, or hand us the whole project from plot to keys.

Service index

Everything, at a glance

A plain list of what we do, what each service covers and who it is for.

No.ServiceWhat is included
01

Engineering design

Plot owners, builders, institutions

Structural design, analysis and working drawings for homes, commercial buildings and institutions, done to code and checked before anything is built.

  • RCC structural design
  • Steel structural design
  • MEP: mechanical, electrical and plumbing layouts
  • Fire fighting design
  • Heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC)
  • RCC detailing
  • Steel detailing
  • Geotechnical investigation and soil testing
02

Architecture

Families building a home

Space planning, elevations, 3D views and interiors, designed around how your family lives and what your plot and the bylaws allow.

  • Building planning
  • Interior design
  • 3D visualisation
  • Landscape design
  • Residential layout planning
03

Government approvals

Anyone who needs a sanction

We know the offices and the paperwork. We prepare the drawings and documents, file them, and follow up until the approval is in your hands.

  • Blueprint online approval
  • PWD SR estimation
  • Local body plan approvals
04

Loans & finance

Home builders and buyers

Help with construction and mortgage loans, and the estimates and valuation reports banks ask for, so funds are released in step with the work.

  • Bank loan approvals for construction and mortgage
  • Building estimation and valuation reports
05

Construction

Homes, shops, schools, factories

Residential, commercial, institutional and industrial buildings, supervised with quality checks at every stage from the foundation to the housewarming.

  • RCC and steel building construction
  • Layout development: roads, drains, water supply, power
  • Low-cost housing inputs
  • Renovation, retrofitting and rehabilitation
  • Building estimation and BOQ preparation
  • Project quality and site coordination
  • Project management consultancy (PMC)
06

Readymade compound walls

Plots, schools, farms, godowns

Posts go in first, then factory-made aerated concrete panels slide into the slots. Your plot is secured quickly, with a neat finish and very little work on site.

  • Made off-site
  • Quick to install
  • No plastering or curing
  • For any boundary: homes, schools, farms, factories
07

Education & training

Students and practising engineers

Practical teaching from the engineers who run our sites, for students, practising engineers and homeowners.

  • Civil engineering software
  • Estimation, BOQ and valuation to bank standards
  • Site engineer training, basic and advanced
  • Civil engineering subjects for Diploma, BE and M.Tech
  • Material testing, including soil
