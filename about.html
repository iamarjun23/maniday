Our story

One point for every wing of civil engineering

Manidhay Business Services began in 2018 in Chikmagalur. Our family has been in the construction business for three decades, and we started with one service: installing readymade aerated concrete wall panels.

In 2019 we decided to offer a full-time, one-point service across every wing of civil engineering, so that clients no longer have to chase an architect, a structural designer, a licensing agent and a contractor separately.

Today our head office is in Chikmagalur, with a branch in Bangalore.
