About
01How it began

Before the company, there was a family on site

Our family has been in construction for three decades. We grew up around sites and drawings, and we saw how hard building was for the families around us: an architect here, a structural designer there, a licensing agent, a bank, a contractor, and nobody joining it all up.

In 2018 we started Manidhay in Chikmagalur with one small service, readymade concrete walls. Clients kept asking us for more. So in 2019 we brought every wing of civil engineering into one team.

We didn't want to be one more name on your list. We wanted to be the one you call.
