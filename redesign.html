Stage 01 / 08

Survey & feasibility

Before a single line is drawn, we walk the plot with you. The boundary is pegged and strung, levels are taken, and a soil test tells us what the ground can safely carry.

  • Boundary and level survey checked against your documents
  • Soil test to choose the right type and depth of foundation
  • Setbacks and buildable area under local building rules
  • An honest early view of what the land can hold and what it will cost
You receive
Site report, soil test results, early budget
Your part
One free site visit with our engineer

Stage 02 / 08

Design

Architects and structural engineers work on the same set of drawings. The blue outline you see is the plan: the house exists on paper, to scale, before anything is built.

  • Floor plans and elevations around your family, plot and vastu
  • Structural design: column grid, footing sizes, beams and steel
  • 3D views so you can see the house before it exists
  • An itemised estimate tied to the approved drawings
You receive
Floor plans, elevations, structural drawings
Your part
Design reviews and a final sign-off

Stage 03 / 08

Approvals & finance

We prepare and file the paperwork, follow it up with the authorities, and give your bank an estimate it accepts. You sign the applications. We do the running between offices.

  • Plan sanction from the local authority
  • NOCs and licences as your site requires
  • Bank-ready estimate and home loan assistance
  • A stage-wise payment plan linked to work done
You receive
Sanctioned plan, NOCs, bank-ready estimate
Your part
Signing the applications

Stage 04 / 08

Foundation

The part nobody sees later, so we do it most carefully. Footings are sized from the soil report, and the steel is inspected before every pour.

  • Column grid marked on site from the structural drawing
  • Excavation and a levelled concrete bed
  • Reinforced footings with column starters
  • Plinth beams tie everything together at ground level
You receive
Photo record of steel before each pour
Your part
Foundation sign-off, first stage payment

Stage 05 / 08

Structure

The reinforced concrete frame rises one floor at a time. Each slab is checked by our engineer before the next level begins.

  • Columns cast exactly to the structural drawings
  • Slab steel, cover and shuttering checked before pouring
  • Concrete compacted and properly cured
  • The next floor starts only after inspection
You receive
Slab-by-slab updates and photos
Your part
Sign-off and payment per slab

Stage 06 / 08

Walls & openings

Masonry fills the frame and rooms take shape. Wiring and plumbing lines go in before plastering, so nothing gets broken open later.

  • Block or brick walls laid to the floor plan
  • Lintels over every door and window opening
  • Concealed electrical conduits and plumbing lines
  • A walk-through with you to check room sizes on site
You receive
Regular site updates
Your part
Room-size check and stage sign-off

Stage 07 / 08

Finishing

Plaster, flooring, paint and fittings, then the outside. Our precast compound wall goes up in days, not weeks, with the gate and landscaping after.

  • Plastering, waterproofing and flooring
  • Paint, fixtures and fittings
  • Precast concrete compound wall and gate
  • Every finish inspected before it's signed off
You receive
Inspected finishes, room by room
Your part
Approving finishes and fittings

Stage 08 / 08

The keys are yours.

A final walkthrough with you, room by room. Then the keys and every document, in one file. On schedule, on budget, with one name accountable from the first peg to the last light.

  • Final walkthrough and snag check
  • Keys, drawings and approvals handed over
  • One point of contact the whole way

Questions

Frequently asked.

Can't find your answer? Call +91 00000 00000 and an engineer will talk you through it.

Do I have to take all seven services?

No. Many clients start with a single service, such as plan approval or structural design, and add others later. Every service can be taken on its own.

Is the first site visit really free?

Yes. We visit your plot, understand what you want to build and give you an honest early view of feasibility and budget, with no obligation.

Can you help me get a home loan?

Yes. We prepare bank-ready estimates and stage-wise cost plans, and work with your bank through the disbursement stages.

How do payments work during construction?

Payments are tied to completed stages, such as foundation, each slab and finishing, so you only pay for work that is done and inspected.

How long does a typical home take?

It depends on size, design and approvals. After the first site visit we give you a written, stage-by-stage schedule so you know exactly what to expect.

Which areas do you work in?

We work across Karnataka. Get in touch with your plot location and we'll confirm.
