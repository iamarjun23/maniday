Who we are

Manidhay began in 2018 in Chikmagalur, where coffee was first grown in India, installing readymade aerated concrete walls. Our family has been in construction for three decades. In 2019 we became a full civil engineering service, with our head office in Chikmagalur and a branch in Bangalore.
