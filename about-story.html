Chapter 01 30+

years our family has spent in construction, long before Manidhay had a name.

Three decades of building

Our family has been in the construction business for three decades. We grew up around sites, drawings and the people who make buildings happen.

We also saw how hard it was for a family to build a home: too many people to deal with, too many offices to visit, and nobody who could explain why each step mattered.

We didn't want to be one more name on that list. We wanted to be the one you call.

Chapter 02 2018

Manidhay Business Services begins in Chikmagalur.

One wall, one town

We started in Chikmagalur, the town where coffee was first grown in India, with a single service: installing readymade aerated concrete wall panels.

Panels made off-site, slotted between posts, finished in days. No plastering, no curing. Schools, godowns and companies trusted us with their boundaries first.

One wall.

Chapter 03 2019

We become a full, one-point civil engineering service.

Six phone numbers, or one

On every wall job we heard the same thing. To build, a family had to find an architect, a structural designer, a licensing agent, a bank and a contractor, and then keep them all talking to each other.

So in 2019 we brought every wing of civil engineering under one roof.
